Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

This portal is a private tool for the strata committee of 9A Cambridge Street. It is run by the committee for its own members, and is not a commercial service.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, the portal reads your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. This happens entirely in your own browser.

What we do with it

Your email address is checked against the list of current committee members to decide whether to show you the portal. That is the only use.

Where it is stored

Nowhere. This site has no server and no database, so none of your information is sent to us or to anyone else. Your sign-in is kept in your own browser's local storage so you are not asked to sign in on every visit. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Documents and the to-do list

The documents and to-do list shown in the portal are stored in Google Drive and Google Sheets, and are governed by Google's privacy policy and by the sharing settings on those files. The portal only displays them, it does not copy or process their contents.

What we do not do

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is shared with any third party.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to secretary.9a.cambridge.st@gmail.com.
